Frequently Asked Questions
Why is antimatter priced at $62 trillion per gram?
Current production costs at facilities like CERN are astronomical because only nanograms have ever been created, requiring massive particle accelerators and years of operation. The $62T figure is an estimated economic cost per gram, not a market price.
What makes rhodium so expensive compared to gold?
Rhodium is one of the rarest platinum-group metals, with annual production around 30 tonnes versus ~3,000 tonnes of gold. Its primary use in catalytic converters creates inelastic industrial demand, pushing prices to roughly $400/g.
How is diamond priced per gram?
Gem-quality diamonds average around $55,000 per gram (≈$275 per carat), though prices vary wildly by cut, color, and clarity. Industrial diamonds are far cheaper—this metric refers to high-grade gemstones.
What is Californium-252 used for?
Cf-252 is a powerful neutron emitter used in portable neutron sources for oil well logging, neutron activation analysis, and cancer therapy. Its $27 million per gram price reflects the extreme difficulty of production in high-flux nuclear reactors.
How does the logarithmic scale help compare costs?
Because antimatter is about 939 million times more expensive than gold, a linear chart would squash all other bars into invisibility. A log₁₀ scale compresses the enormous range into a readable, visually meaningful comparison.
