❓ FAQ

What does this simulation model? +
It compares the cumulative cost of a daily $5 coffee habit against investing that same amount into Bitcoin at a chosen annual appreciation rate.
How are the BTC returns calculated? +
We apply a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) from the slider, starting from 2010 with an initial $5,000 position. The daily contribution is also invested at the same rate.
What is the "Inflation Adjustment"? +
It slightly discounts the future value of the coffee spending to reflect purchasing power changes over time, but does not affect the BTC portfolio.
Can I export the data? +
Yes — use the "Export CSV" button to download a full telemetry file with year-by-year breakdowns.
