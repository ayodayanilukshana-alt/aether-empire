Frequently Asked Questions

Is Monster Beverage stock a good long-term investment?

Past performance does not guarantee future results. The simulation uses a customizable annual return slider. The base preset (25%) reflects the historical approximate CAGR of MNST over the 15-year period. Always conduct independent research.

How is the wealth gap calculated?

Wealth Gap = Stock Value − Coffee Cumulative Spend. A positive number means the stock investment outpaced the coffee habit; a negative number means the habit cost more than the investment gained.

What does "15-Yr Shock" mean in this context?

The title references the surprising outcome over a 15-year horizon: a modest $5/day coffee habit can cost a significant amount, while investing the same amount in a high-growth stock like Monster Beverage could yield a dramatically different result. The simulation lets you explore the range of outcomes.
