Frequently Asked Questions

What is the opportunity cost of a daily coffee?

The opportunity cost is the potential future value of the money spent on coffee if it were invested instead. At $5/day for 15 years, you'd spend $27,375 on coffee, but that same amount invested in Nvidia could grow to over $118,000 based on our simulation model.

How is the Nvidia portfolio value calculated?

We use a staged growth model derived from the provided data series, which reflects historical Nvidia stock performance. The portfolio value compounds annually based on the investment amount, adjusted for the time horizon.

Can I adjust the daily amount and years?

Yes. Use the sliders to change the daily coffee spend and investment horizon. The results update instantly, including the canvas chart, KPIs, and breakdown table.
