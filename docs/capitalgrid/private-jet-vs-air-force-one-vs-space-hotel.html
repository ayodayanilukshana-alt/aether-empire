Frequently asked questions

What does the "Allocation weight" slider represent?
It sets a subjective weighting for each asset in the opulence index. Higher weight increases the asset's contribution to the canvas visualization and the notional value calculation. Presets provide quick scenarios.
How is the "Notional value" calculated?
Each asset has a baseline notional value derived from its headline metric (e.g., $100M private jet, $20B ultimate price tag). The slider weight scales that baseline relative to the sum of all weights, creating a dynamic capital allocation view.
Can I export my scenario?
Yes. Use "Export CSV Telemetry" to download the current weights, opulence units, and notional values. Use "Copy Scenario Link" to copy a shareable URL with the slider positions encoded.
What is the ultimate price tag?
The $20B ultimate price tag represents a hypothetical combined valuation of all flying palaces and the space hotel suite, used as a capstone metric in the data payload.
