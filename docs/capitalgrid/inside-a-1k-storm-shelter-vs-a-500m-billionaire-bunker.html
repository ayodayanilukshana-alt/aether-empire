Frequently Asked Questions

What defines the Survivability Index?

The index combines area per person, threat severity, and duration. Higher is better. It is normalized so the billionaires’ bunker peaks near 98–100 under extreme threat.

How is breathing space calculated?

Breathing space = total area (sq ft) divided by occupancy. The $1K shelter yields about 2 sq ft / person under default settings, while the $500M bunker exceeds 40,000 sq ft / person.

Can I export the telemetry?

Yes — click “Export CSV Telemetry” to download a CSV containing all tier metrics along with your current slider configuration and survivability scores.

Why is the canvas log-scaled?

Because the price and area differences span six orders of magnitude (from $1K to $500M). A log scale keeps all five tiers visible and comparable.
