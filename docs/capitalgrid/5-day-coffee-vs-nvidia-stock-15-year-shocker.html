Analytical FAQ

Why is the Nvidia series non-linear?
The provided series_b reflects compounding growth and volatility typical of high-growth equities, especially over a 15-year horizon. The simulation scales it via the growth multiplier.
How is the coffee habit calculated?
The base series_a is cumulative spend over 6 representative periods (scaled to 15 years). We use a daily spend slider to adjust proportionally.
What does the growth multiplier do?
It linearly scales the Nvidia investment values (series_b) to model more aggressive or conservative growth assumptions.
Can I export the adjusted telemetry?
Yes. The Export CSV button downloads a file with the current adjusted series for both assets.
