Frequently Asked Questions

What does the simulation actually compare?

It compares the cumulative cash spent on a daily $5 coffee habit against investing that identical daily cash flow into Costco (COST) stock. The canvas overlays cumulative coffee spend, invested principal, and the compounded portfolio value over your selected horizon.

Where does the "15-Year Shock" number come from?

The video's reference grid ({5000, 6200, 7800, 9500, 12400, 16800} for coffee-side cumulative vs {5000, 9400, 6100, 22000, 54000, 118000} for the stock path) is echoed by the "Video Snapshot" preset, which reproduces its growth profile so you can explore the drivers behind the divergence.

Why does the portfolio dip at some points?

Volatile early returns and the way principal accumulates can produce interim drawdowns in the reference path. The simulation lets you isolate the effect of annual return, compounding frequency, and deploy interval on the final outcome.

How is inflation applied?

The Inflation Drag slider discounts the nominal portfolio value to a real purchasing-power basis, drawn as the grey line on the canvas. Coffee spend is treated as nominal cash outlays for the direct comparison.

Can I export the data?

Yes. "Export CSV Telemetry" downloads a CSV containing the full yearly schedule plus your active parameter set. "Copy Scenario Link" writes a permalink (with URL params) so you can reload the exact configuration.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is an educational simulation workstation. Past growth profiles of any stock do not guarantee future results. Consult a licensed professional before investing.
