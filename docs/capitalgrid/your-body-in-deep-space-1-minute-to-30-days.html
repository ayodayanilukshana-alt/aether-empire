Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to the human body in the first 15 seconds of vacuum exposure?
Depressurization causes rapid swelling, ebullism (boiling of bodily fluids), and hypoxia. Consciousness is lost within ~15 seconds.
Why is "boiling blood" used to describe the 1‑minute mark?
At near‑zero pressure, the boiling point of water drops below body temperature. Blood and saliva literally vaporize, causing massive tissue expansion.
What does "frozen desiccation" at 24 hours mean?
In deep space, the body loses heat via radiation and water sublimates directly from ice to vapor, leaving a desiccated, mummified remains.
How does orbital decay lead to re‑entry after 7 days?
Without propulsion, a body in low Earth orbit experiences drag and its altitude decreases until it re‑enters the atmosphere.
