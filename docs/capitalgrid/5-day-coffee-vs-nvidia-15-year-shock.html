🧠 Frequently Asked Questions
What is the "$5/Day Coffee vs Nvidia" backtest? +
This simulation compares the opportunity cost of spending $5 daily on coffee versus investing that same amount into Nvidia (NVDA) stock over a 15-year horizon. It models both the savings trajectory (with interest) and the equity growth trajectory (with compounding returns).
Why does NVDA show such dramatic growth? +
Nvidia has experienced extraordinary returns driven by the AI boom, GPU demand, and data center expansion. The base case uses a 38% annualized return, which reflects its historical 15-year performance. However, past performance does not guarantee future results.
How do I use the scenario presets? +
Presets instantly adjust the sliders to predefined configurations: "Conservative" uses lower returns, "Base Case" matches the video's assumptions, "Aggressive" assumes higher NVDA returns, and "2008 Shock" simulates a severe market downturn followed by recovery.
Can I export the data for my own analysis? +
Yes. Click the "Export CSV Telemetry" button to download a CSV file containing the year-by-year breakdown based on your current slider settings. The "Copy Scenario Link" button encodes your parameters into the URL for easy sharing.
