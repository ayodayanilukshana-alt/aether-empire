Frequently Asked Questions
What is the core premise of this backtest?
This simulation compares the opportunity cost of a daily $5 coffee habit versus investing that same $5 daily into Nvidia stock over a 15-year period. It uses historical and hypothetical return assumptions to project terminal wealth.
How are the Nvidia returns calculated?
The model uses a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) slider, defaulting to 30% to approximate Nvidia's historic performance, but allows you to adjust between 0% and 50% to test different market environments.
What does the "Net Difference" KPI represent?
It is the final Nvidia portfolio value minus the total cumulative coffee spend. A positive number indicates the investment strategy outperformed the coffee habit.
Can I export the data for my own analysis?
Yes, the "Export CSV Telemetry" button downloads a CSV containing the year-by-year breakdown of coffee spend, Nvidia value, and delta based on your current slider settings.
What is the "Copy Scenario Link" feature?
It copies a URL that includes your current slider parameters (coffee price and return rate) as query strings, allowing you to share your specific simulation setup with others.
