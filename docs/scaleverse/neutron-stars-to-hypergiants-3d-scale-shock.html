Frequently Asked Questions

What is the smallest object in this simulation?
The Quark Star (Strange Star) with a diameter of 12 kilometers, followed closely by the Neutron Star at 20 km.
Why is a logarithmic scale used?
The size difference between a quark star and a hypergiant spans over 8 orders of magnitude, making a linear scale impractical for visual comparison.
What is the largest object shown?
The Yellow Hypergiant (VY Canis Majoris) with a diameter of 2 billion kilometers.
Can I export the data?
Yes, use the "Export CSV Telemetry" button to download a CSV file containing all object names, diameters, and computed visual scales.
