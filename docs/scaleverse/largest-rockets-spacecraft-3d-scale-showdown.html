FAQ

What does the zoom slider do?
It magnifies the canvas view without altering the true relative scale of the rockets. Use it to inspect smaller vehicles like the V-2.
How is the "Relative to V-2" column calculated?
It divides each rocket's height by 14 m (the V-2 height) to show how many V-2 stacks would fit inside.
Can I export the data?
Yes, use the "Export CSV Telemetry" button to download a CSV with all rocket names, heights, and computed ratios.
What is the "Copy Scenario Link" button?
It copies the current URL with your zoom and offset settings pre-applied, so you can share the exact view.
