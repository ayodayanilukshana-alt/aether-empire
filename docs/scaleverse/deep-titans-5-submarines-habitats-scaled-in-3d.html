◈ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How is the scale comparison calculated?

Each vessel's length in meters is plotted proportionally on the canvas. The longest vessel (Belgorod, 184m) sets the reference, and all others are scaled relative to it. The zoom slider adjusts the horizontal rendering scale across all vessels equally.

What is the source of the length data?

Lengths are compiled from public naval engineering records and historical specifications: Alvin (DSV-2) 7.1m, SEALAB II 17m, K-278 Komsomolets 118m, Typhoon-class 175m, and Project 210 Belgorod 184m. Conversions to feet use 1m = 3.28084ft.

Can I export the telemetry data?

Yes. Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV containing each vessel's name, length in meters, feet, and relative scale factor. The button generates the file dynamically from the current dataset.

What does the "Copy Scenario Link" button do?

It copies a URL to your clipboard that encodes the current zoom and highlight state, allowing you to share a specific visualization configuration. The link includes query parameters for zoom and highlight index.

Is this simulation based on the video?

This workstation directly models the five vessels highlighted in the YouTube video "Deep Titans: 5 Submarines & Habitats Scaled in 3D". The embedded player above provides the source documentary context.
