Frequently Asked Questions
Why is Indianapolis Motor Speedway so much larger?

The 4,000 m span models the full 2.5-mile (≈4,023 m) oval circuit, a motorsport venue rather than a traditional ballpark. Its "seats" metric aggregates all permanent and temporary grandstand capacity along the circuit.

How is seat density calculated?

Seat density divides total seats by the venue's linear span in meters, giving a normalized comparative index. Fenway Park's compact footprint yields higher density, while the speedway's vast perimeter lowers its ratio.

Can I copy a scenario link?

Yes — the "Copy Scenario Link" button serializes your current scale lens, label visibility, and active preset into the URL hash so you can share the exact viewport configuration.

What does the telemetry CSV contain?

The export includes arena names, span meters, seating capacity, computed density, relative scale index, and the active lens configuration at time of export.
