FAQ

Why is the LHC so large in comparison?
The Large Hadron Collider is a 27 km ring, making it 54,000 times longer than the Deep Space Atomic Clock. Our log‑scale visualisation compresses the range to fit within the canvas while preserving relative differences.
What does "size emphasis" do?
It applies a power‑law exponent to the raw size values before visual scaling, allowing you to exaggerate or compress the differences between objects. A value of 1.0 keeps the standard logarithmic projection.
How is total cost calculated?
We sum the approximate cost figures: DSAC $120M, Apollo LM $150M, Hubble $10B, JWST $10B, LHC $9B = $29.27 billion. Decimal values are estimated from the "metric" strings.
