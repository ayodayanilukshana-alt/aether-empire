Analytical FAQ

How does the Bagger 293 compare to a standard loader?

The Bagger 293 towers at 96 meters — over 34 times taller than the Epiroc Scooptram ST14 (2.8 m). It is one of the largest land vehicles ever built and requires a crew of five to operate its massive bucket wheel.

What does the "Relative Scale" metric represent?

Relative Scale is calculated by dividing each machine's primary metric by the smallest machine (Epiroc Scooptram ST14 at 2.8 m). This provides a normalized comparison factor across the fleet.

Why are different metrics used (height vs. length)?

The dataset tracks the most defining dimension for each machine: height for the Bagger 293 and Komatsu 930E, length for the Liebherr T 282B, and boom length for the Bucyrus-Erie 1850-B.

Can I export this data for external analysis?

Yes. Use the "Export CSV" button to download a comma-separated file containing the exact DATA PAYLOAD values, including scale multipliers and derived ratios.

What is the maximum scale multiplier in the viewport?

The slider allows scaling from 0.5× to 2.0×. At 2.0×, the largest machine (Bagger 293) is rendered at an effective scale of 192 meters in the simulated viewport.
