What is the purpose of this simulation?
It visualizes the extreme size differences between stellar remnants (neutron star, white dwarf) and hypergiants (Betelgeuse, VY Canis Majoris, UY Scuti) using accurate diameter data.
How are the diameters scaled in the canvas?
A logarithmic scale is used to fit objects spanning 8 orders of magnitude, with a user-controlled scale factor and preset filters.
Can I export the data?
Yes, click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV containing all current object metrics and scenario parameters.
What does the scale factor slider do?
It multiplies the displayed size of all objects, allowing you to zoom in or out of the visualization without changing the underlying data.
