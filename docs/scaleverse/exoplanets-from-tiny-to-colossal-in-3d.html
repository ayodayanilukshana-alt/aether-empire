❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the size range of these exoplanets?
From Kepler-37b (3,900 km) to GQ Lupi b (2.5 million km) — a staggering factor of ~641x.
How is the 3D visualization scaled?
Radii are normalized to the largest object (GQ Lupi b) and adjusted by the zoom slider.
Can I export the telemetry data?
Yes, use the "Export CSV Telemetry" button to download a CSV of all planet diameters.
