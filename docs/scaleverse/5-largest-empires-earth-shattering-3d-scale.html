Frequently Asked Questions
How is the 3D sphere size calculated?

Sphere volume (and visual radius) is proportional to the cube root of land area to maintain volume-preserving scaling. The Aztec Empire (500,000 km²) serves as the baseline reference. Larger empires like the British Empire (35,500,000 km²) appear dramatically larger due to the cubic relationship.

What do the slider controls actually affect?

Zoom adjusts the camera distance, Rotation Speed controls the auto-spin of the 3D spheres, Opacity changes the transparency of the rendered spheres, and the Aztec Reference Size slider scales the baseline sphere to help compare relative volumes visually.

Why are the empires arranged in this specific order?

The spheres are arranged from smallest to largest land area in a horizontal sequence: Aztec, Roman, Russian, Mongol, and British. This mirrors the ascending scale narrative from the source video and makes the magnitude differences immediately apparent.

Can I export the current simulation state?

Yes — the "Export CSV Telemetry" button generates a CSV file containing the current slider values and the underlying land-area dataset. The "Copy Scenario Link" button copies a shareable URL that encodes your current slider configuration.
