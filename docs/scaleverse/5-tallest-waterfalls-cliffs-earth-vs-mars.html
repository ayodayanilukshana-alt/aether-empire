FREQUENTLY ASKED

How does Olympus Mons compare to Verona Rupes?
Olympus Mons stands at 22,000 m, while Verona Rupes is 20,000 m. Both are on Mars, but Verona Rupes is a cliff, whereas Olympus Mons is a shield volcano.
What is the tallest waterfall on Earth in this dataset?
Salto del Angel (Angel Falls) at 979 m — the world's highest uninterrupted waterfall.
Why is Valles Marineris included?
Its wall reaches 4,000 m — a massive cliff system on Mars, far exceeding any Earth waterfall drop.
