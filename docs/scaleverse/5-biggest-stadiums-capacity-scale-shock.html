Frequently Asked Questions

What does the "Capacity Scale Multiplier" do?
It proportionally scales every stadium's displayed capacity and bar height. A multiplier of 2.0× doubles the modelled capacity, allowing you to explore hypothetical scenarios.
How is "Capacity / Meter" calculated?
It divides the scaled capacity by the linear meters provided in the dataset. This metric helps compare spatial efficiency across venues.
Can I export the current scenario?
Yes. Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV containing the current scaled capacities and parameters. "Copy Scenario Link" generates a shareable URL with the multiplier encoded.
What is the source of the stadium data?
The dataset matches the video's exact payload: five stadiums with their base capacities and linear dimensions. No external data is used.
