Analytical FAQ

Why does the LHC ring dominate the scale?
At 27 km circumference it is 250× longer than the ISS width. The visualization uses logarithmic compression to keep all objects visible.
What does the cost per meter indicate?
AMS-02: $400M/m, LHC dipole: $667M/m, JWST: $476M/m, ISS: $1.38B/m, LHC ring: $0.37M/m. High precision small objects cost more per meter.
Can I export the current view?
Yes. The CSV telemetry includes the current scale factor, active preset, and per-object costs.
