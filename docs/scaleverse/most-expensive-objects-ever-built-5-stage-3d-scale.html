FAQ

What is SCALEVERSE 3D?
A 3D scale comparison workstation for visualizing the relative sizes of the most expensive objects ever built. Adjust zoom, perspective, and spacing to explore.
How are the objects rendered?
The canvas uses an isometric 3D projection. Each object is drawn as a labeled block with a height proportional to its real-world length in metres.
Why is the Large Hadron Collider so large?
The LHC is a 27 km ring of superconducting magnets. At this scale, it dwarfs all other objects—its length is over 54,000× the chess set.
Can I export the data?
Yes. Click "CSV Telemetry" to download a spreadsheet of all object lengths, costs, and ratios. Use "Copy Scenario Link" to share the current viewer state.
