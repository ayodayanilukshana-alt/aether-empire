Frequently asked questions

What does the visualization represent?

It presents a stylized, top-down silhouette whose horizontal span is proportional to the selected flyer’s supplied wingspan. All subjects share one common scale, so their sizes can be compared directly.

Which flyer has the greatest listed wingspan?

Quetzalcoatlus northropi leads this dataset at 11.0 meters, followed by Pelagornis sandersi at 7.4 meters.

What do the scenario presets change?

Presets select a subject or group of subjects and update the display. The zoom and rotation controls adjust the visualization only; they do not modify the source measurements.

What is included in the CSV export?

The export records all five supplied measurements along with the current selected flyer, view scale, and silhouette rotation.
