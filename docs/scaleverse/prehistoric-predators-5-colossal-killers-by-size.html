Frequently Asked Questions
What is the largest predator in this dataset?
Spinosaurus is the largest at 15 meters in length, according to the provided data. It is followed closely by Tyrannosaurus Rex and Deinosuchus, both at 12 meters.
How does the scale multiplier work?
The scale multiplier linearly scales all predator lengths. A multiplier of 2.0× doubles every predator's size, while 0.5× halves them. The relative proportions between species remain constant.
Can I export the data for analysis?
Yes. Click the "Export CSV Telemetry" button in the header to download a CSV file containing the original lengths, scaled lengths, and ratios for all five predators at the current scale setting.
What does the size ratio indicate?
The size ratio compares the scaled length of the largest predator (Spinosaurus) to the smallest (Velociraptor). At 1.0× scale, the ratio is 10:1 (15m vs 1.5m). This ratio remains constant regardless of the scale multiplier.
How do I share a specific scenario?
Use the "Copy Scenario Link" button. It encodes the current scale value into the URL hash (e.g., #scale=2.5). When you share that link, the simulation will automatically load with that scale.
