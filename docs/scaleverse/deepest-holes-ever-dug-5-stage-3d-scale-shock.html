Frequently Asked Questions

How deep is the Kola Superdeep Borehole?
The Kola Superdeep Borehole reaches 12,262 meters (12.26 km) — the deepest human-made hole on Earth.
What is the Woodingdean Well?
A hand-dug well in Brighton, UK, reaching 390 meters (1,280 ft). It is the deepest hand-dug well in the world.
Which is deeper: Mponeng or TauTona?
TauTona Mine is slightly deeper at 3.9 km (3,900 m) compared to Mponeng's 3.6 km (3,600 m).
What is the Kidd Mine depth?
The Kidd Mine in Canada reaches approximately 3,000 meters (3.0 km) below the surface.
