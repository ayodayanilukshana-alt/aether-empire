Frequently Asked Questions
How are the organisms ordered in the simulation?
They are ordered by their physical scale in meters, from smallest (Old Tjikko at 5 m) to largest (Posidonia oceanica clone at 15,000 m / 15 km). The canvas visualizes them proportionally with a dynamic zoom system.
What does "Scale Factor" mean in the table?
It represents the relative size ratio compared to the smallest organism (Old Tjikko, 5 m). For example, Pando has a scale factor of 26× because its clonal colony spans 130 m.
Can I export the current telemetry?
Yes. Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV file containing all organism data, current zoom level, offset position, and timestamp. The data is generated live from the simulation state.
How does "Copy Scenario Link" work?
It copies a URL to your clipboard that includes the current zoom and offset parameters as query strings. Opening that link will restore the exact viewport state when the page loads.
Why is the Posidonia clone shown at 15 km but labeled 15,000 m?
The metric uses meters for consistency across all organisms. 15,000 meters equals 15 kilometers, which is the estimated width of the Posidonia oceanica clone discovered in the Mediterranean.
