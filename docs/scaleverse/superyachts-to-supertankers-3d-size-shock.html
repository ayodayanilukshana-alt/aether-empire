FAQ · SCALE INSIGHTS
Why is Prelude FLNG the largest?
Prelude FLNG measures 488 m (1,601 ft) in length and is the largest floating facility ever built, designed for offshore gas extraction. It is 30 m longer than the Seawise Giant.
How does Azzam compare to a supertanker?
Azzam at 142 m is roughly 3.4× shorter than Prelude FLNG. The Batillus supertanker (414 m) is nearly three times the length of Azzam.
What does the "Ratio vs Azzam" column show?
It displays how many times longer each vessel is compared to the baseline superyacht Azzam (142 m). A value of 3.44 means the vessel is 3.44 times longer.
