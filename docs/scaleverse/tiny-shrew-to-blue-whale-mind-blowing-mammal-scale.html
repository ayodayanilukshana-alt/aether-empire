FAQ · Scale Insights

How does the shrew compare to the blue whale? +
An Etruscan shrew (0.036 m) is roughly 830× shorter than a blue whale (29.9 m), and about 75 million times lighter (2 g vs 150,000 kg).
What is the scale factor? +
The scale factor is the linear ratio vs the shrew. Whale / shrew = 29.9 / 0.036 ≈ 830.6× length.
How are masses estimated? +
We use published body masses: shrew ~2g, rabbit 2kg, anteater 33kg, elephant 6000kg, blue whale 150,000kg.
