FAQ

What does the 3D size loop show?

It visualizes the height of history's largest rockets, from the V-2 (14 m) to SpaceX Starship (121 m), alongside the Statue of Unity (182 m) for scale. The yaw slider lets you rotate the 3D representation.

How is the reference height used?

The slider sets the maximum height drawn on the canvas (default 182 m). All vehicles are scaled proportionally, so you can compare them against any reference.

Can I export the data?

Yes — click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV with vehicle names, heights in meters and feet, and ratio versus V-2.

Is this simulation physically accurate?

The heights are based on public specifications. The 3D rendering is a stylized comparison tool, not an engineering blueprint.
