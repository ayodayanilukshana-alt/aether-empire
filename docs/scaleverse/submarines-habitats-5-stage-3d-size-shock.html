FAQ
What does the "Scale Factor" slider control? +
The scale factor applies a logarithmic multiplier to the visual rendering of all five objects on the canvas. A higher value stretches the visual comparison, making the largest object (SEALAB III, 3000m) more dominant while still keeping smaller objects visible.
Why is SEALAB III listed as "3,000 m deep" rather than "long"? +
SEALAB III was an underwater habitat designed to operate at a depth of approximately 3,000 meters (its operational depth rating). For a size comparison, we interpret its "size" as the maximum depth it was engineered to reach, which creates a fascinating contrast with the length-based measurements of the submarines.
How is the CSV telemetry generated? +
The CSV export captures the current slider values (scale, depth, clarity), along with the computed KPI metrics and the full dataset of the five items. It is formatted as a standard CSV file ready for spreadsheet analysis.
Does the "Copy Scenario Link" button preserve my slider settings? +
Yes. The link encodes your current scale, depth, and clarity values as URL query parameters. When opened, the workstation will automatically restore your exact configuration.
