Frequently asked questions
How is the vertical scale calculated?
All rockets are drawn to real-world proportions, with the tallest (Starship, 121 m) fitting the canvas height. The “vertical exaggeration” slider applies a uniform multiplier to emphasise differences.
Why does the V-2 look so small?
The V-2 stands 14 metres tall—roughly the height of a four-story building. Compared to Starship (121 m), it is just 11.6% as tall. The visualisation highlights this dramatic evolutionary leap.
What do the KPI readouts represent?
Tallest/shortest heights in metres, the height ratio between the two extremes, and the arithmetic mean of all five rockets. These update automatically when you filter presets.
Can I export the data for research?
Yes. Click “Export CSV Telemetry” to download a spreadsheet containing rocket names, heights, and computed ratios.
