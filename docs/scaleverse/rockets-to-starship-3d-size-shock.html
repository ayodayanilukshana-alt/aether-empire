Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is the Starship compared to the Saturn V?
Starship stands at 120 meters, making it roughly 9 meters taller than the Saturn V (111 m). That's about an 8% increase in height.
What is the smallest rocket in this comparison?
The V-2 Rocket, at 14 meters, is the smallest. It's about 8.5 times shorter than the Starship.
Are these heights accurate?
Yes, the data reflects standard published heights for each vehicle, including the Space Shuttle stack (56 m) and Falcon 9 (70 m).
How does the visualization work?
The canvas renders each rocket as a stylized 3D bar, scaling height proportionally. You can rotate the view by dragging and zoom with your scroll wheel.
