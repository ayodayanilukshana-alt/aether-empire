Analytical FAQ
How does the scale comparison work?
All items are rendered proportionally to their real‑world length in meters. The Typhoon‑class submarine (175 m) sets the maximum reference width.
Why does depth slider affect perspective?
Simulates camera distance – lower values create a “macro” close‑up, higher values show the whole fleet.
What is the CSV export content?
Includes name, meters, metric, and current visual scale factor for all five vehicles/habitats.
Can I share my scenario?
Yes – “Copy Scenario Link” encodes current slider values into the URL hash.
