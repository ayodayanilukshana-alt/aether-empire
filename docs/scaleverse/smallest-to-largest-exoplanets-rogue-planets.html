❓ Frequently asked questions

Why is Kepler-37b so small?
It is slightly larger than the Moon, orbiting a distant star. Its diameter is only 3,860 km — smaller than Mercury.
How does WASP-17b compare to Jupiter?
WASP-17b has a diameter of ~268,000 km, nearly twice that of Jupiter. It is one of the most inflated exoplanets known.
What is a rogue planet?
A rogue planet is a planetary-mass object that does not orbit a star. 2MASS J2126-8140 is a massive rogue planet with a diameter of 284,000 km.
