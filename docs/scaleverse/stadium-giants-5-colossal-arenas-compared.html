Frequently Asked Questions

How is the 3D scale calculated?
Each venue's block height is proportional to its seat capacity relative to the largest venue (257,000). The scale slider further exaggerates or compresses the visual representation for comparison.
Can I export the current scenario?
Yes, use the "Export CSV Telemetry" button to download a CSV file containing venue names, capacities, and computed relative metrics based on the current view scale.
What does "Compare Mode" do?
It resets the view scale to 1.0× and ensures all blocks are visible at their exact proportional heights, ideal for direct visual magnitude comparison.
Is this data real?
Yes — capacities reflect publicly listed seating capacities for these iconic venues, as referenced in the video.
