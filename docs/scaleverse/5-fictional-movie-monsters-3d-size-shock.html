FAQ · SCALE & COMPARISON
How does the simulation handle extreme size differences?
We apply logarithmic compression to the visual axis, preserving relative order while making all 5 monsters visible. Cthulhu at 3,200 m is ~2,133× taller than the 1.5 m Critter.
What does the "Scale Compression" slider do?
It visually expands or contracts the drawn heights without altering the underlying numerical data. The KPI readouts always reflect real metric values.
Can I download the data?
Yes — click "Export CSV Telemetry" to get a CSV with all monster names, meters, feet, and ratio values relative to the human reference.
