FAQ

What is the fossil ball maze duel?
A physics-based rivalry simulation where a Velociraptor and a Triceratops navigate a maze using fossil balls, competing for score based on collisions and positional control.
How are the telemetry KPIs calculated?
Raptor and Trike scores are computed from successful maze exits and ball interactions. Collisions and time are tracked per frame. Export the CSV for raw data.
Can I adjust the simulation parameters?
Yes. Use the sliders for Raptor Agility, Trike Momentum, and Fossil Friction. Presets load tuned configurations. The canvas updates in real time.
Is the YouTube stream live?
The embedded stream is the original broadcast of the Fossil Ball Maze Duel. Use the playback controls to review.
