❓ FAQ

What is the Sushi vs Tacos Ball Maze simulation?

A 60FPS rigid-body physics rivalry simulation where two teams — SUSHI and TACOS — compete in an enclosed maze environment. Balls bounce, collide, and accumulate telemetry across runs.

How does the scoring work?

Each ball accumulates bounce counts. The team with the higher cumulative bounce count is declared the winner. The simulation resets after 60 seconds or manual reset.

What does the CSV export contain?

The CSV telemetry includes timestamp, gravity, bounce, friction, sushi mass, tacos mass, sushi bounces, tacos bounces, sushi velocity, tacos velocity, sushi KE, and tacos KE.

Can I copy the current scenario configuration?

Yes. Click "COPY SCENARIO LINK" to copy a URL containing all current slider values. Paste it into a browser to restore the exact configuration.

What is KINETICZEN LABS?

KINETICZEN LABS is a creative engineering studio building interactive physics simulations, calculators, and 3D sandbox experiences. Visit the portal.
