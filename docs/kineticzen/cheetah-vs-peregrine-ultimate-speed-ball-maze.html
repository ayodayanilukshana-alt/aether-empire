❓ FAQ
What does the simulation model?
A 60 FPS rigid-body ball maze comparing the speed and agility of a cheetah and a peregrine falcon.
How are the KPI values updated?
Sliders adjust live physics parameters: speed, maze complexity, and gravity, which update both the canvas and KPIs.
Can I export telemetry?
Yes. Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download current frame data including positions and velocities.
