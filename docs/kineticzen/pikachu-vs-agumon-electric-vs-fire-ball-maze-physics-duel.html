FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is the Pikachu vs Agumon maze physics duel?
A 60FPS rigid-body rivalry simulation built on Matter.js. Two entities—Pikachu (electric charge) and Agumon (fire intensity)—navigate a procedurally generated maze. Each collision redistributes kinetic energy and affects their respective element meters.
How is the simulation configured?
Adjust sliders for Electric Charge (Pikachu), Fire Intensity (Agumon), Gravity (maze tilt), and Obstacle Density. Presets instantly set tuned parameter sets for repeated experiments.
What telemetry can I export?
The CSV export includes time-series data: velocities, collision counts, energy flux, and entity-specific metrics for Pikachu and Agumon. Copy Scenario Link generates a permalink with current slider state.
Is this based on the YouTube video?
Yes—this workstation is engineered for the exact data payload and visual narrative from the "Pikachu vs Agumon: Electric vs Fire Ball Maze Physics Duel" video. The embedded stream is available on the right.
