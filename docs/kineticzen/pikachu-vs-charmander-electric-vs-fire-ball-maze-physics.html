FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
How does the maze physics simulation work?
Each team is represented by a set of rigid-body balls that bounce, collide, and interact with maze walls. The Electric charge slider scales Pikachu's kinetic energy and collision radius, while Fire intensity does the same for Charmander. The simulation runs at 60 FPS using a simple impulse-based solver.
What do the KPI readouts mean?
Energy (joules) is the sum of kinetic energy of all balls for each team. Collisions count total wall and inter-team impulses. Dominance is the ratio of Pikachu's total energy to Charmander's total energy (1.0 = balanced).
Can I export the telemetry data?
Yes — click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV snapshot of the current state including timestamps, energy values, collision counts, and parameter settings.
How do I share this exact scenario?
Click "Copy Scenario Link" — it copies a URL containing the current slider positions as query parameters. Opening that link reloads the same simulation configuration.
