Frequently Asked Questions

What does the simulation model?
It models a 60FPS rigid-body rivalry between a mantis shrimp and a spider navigating a ball maze. Sliders adjust strike force, agility, and maze complexity, affecting ball movement and final scores.
How are the KPI scores calculated?
Scores are derived from a weighted combination of force, agility, and maze complexity. Higher force and agility yield higher scores, while complexity introduces a dampening factor.
Can I export telemetry data?
Yes. Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV containing the current slider values, KPI scores, and table metrics.
How do I share my scenario?
Click "Copy Scenario Link" — the URL will encode your current slider settings. Share the link to let others reproduce your exact simulation state.
What is the YouTube embed showing?
It embeds the original "Mantis Shrimp vs Spider: Ultimate Ball Maze Duel" video, which served as the data payload for this workstation.
