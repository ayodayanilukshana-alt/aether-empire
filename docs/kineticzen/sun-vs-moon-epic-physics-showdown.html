FAQ

What is this simulation modeling? +
A custom 60FPS rigid-body rivalry between two teams: SUN (gold) and MOON (silver). Sliders control gravity, velocity, and mass; the canvas renders live physics interactions.
How is telemetry generated? +
Each frame computes approximate force vectors, collision energy, and momentum transfer, logged for CSV export. The readouts update in real time.
Can I reproduce a scenario? +
Yes. Use "Copy Scenario Link" to copy a URL with all slider values encoded as query parameters. Share or reload to restore the exact state.
What does the export contain? +
CSV with columns: timestamp, sunForce, moonForce, collisionEnergy, sunVelocity, moonVelocity, sunGravity, moonGravity.
