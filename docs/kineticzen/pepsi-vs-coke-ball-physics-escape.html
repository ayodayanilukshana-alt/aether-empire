◈ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What does the simulation model?

Two rigid circular bodies (PEPSI & COKE) in a bounded container under gravity and elastic collisions. The ‘escape’ refers to a ball leaving the viewport when kinetic energy exceeds the box potential.

How is kinetic energy computed?

KE = ½ m v². Mass is fixed at 1.2 kg per ball. Velocity magnitude includes both x and y components derived from the physics integration.

What do the preset buttons do?

DEFAULT restores baseline values. LOW-G reduces gravity. BOUNCY raises elasticity. HEAD-ON sets symmetric opposite velocities. ESCAPE boosts velocities to force escape.

Can I export the telemetry?

Yes, click EXPORT CSV to download a CSV file containing the current parameters and real-time KPI values for both teams.

How do I share this scenario?

Click COPY LINK to copy the current scenario state (encoded in URL hash) to your clipboard. Paste it into any browser to reproduce exact settings.
