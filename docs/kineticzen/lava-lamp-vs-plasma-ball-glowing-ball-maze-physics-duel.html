FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What physics engine powers this simulation?
This workstation uses a custom lightweight rigid-body integrator designed for 60 FPS performance. It models elastic collisions, viscous drag (lava lamp), and charge-repulsion (plasma ball) within a procedural maze.
How is the 'Lava Lamp' team different from 'Plasma Ball'?
The Lava Lamp team has higher viscosity (slower, more damped motion) and red-tinted balls. The Plasma Ball team has lower damping, higher restitution, and blue-tinted balls with electrostatic charge that adds repulsive forces.
How do I export telemetry?
Click the "Export CSV Telemetry" button. A CSV file will download containing the current simulation parameters, collision counts, energy readings, and per-ball velocity data.
Can I share my exact scenario?
Yes. Click "Copy Scenario Link" to copy a URL containing all slider values and preset configurations. Paste it anywhere to reproduce this exact simulation state.
What do the KPI scores represent?
The score is a weighted combination of successful maze collisions, normalized kinetic energy, and team-specific multipliers (Lava = viscosity bonus, Plasma = charge bonus). Higher is better for that team.
