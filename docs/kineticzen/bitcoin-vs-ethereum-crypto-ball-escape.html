Frequently Asked Questions

What does the simulation model?
Two rigid bodies (BTC and ETH) interact under gravity, restitution, and friction inside a bounded arena. The escape velocity and kinetic energy are computed in real time using Matter.js physics principles.
Can I export telemetry?
Yes. The "Export CSV Telemetry" button generates a CSV file with current physics parameters and live KPI readings.
How is the scenario link generated?
The "Copy Scenario Link" button serializes the current slider values and physics state into the URL hash, allowing you to share an exact reproduction of your configuration.
What is the YouTube stream?
The embedded stream (https://www.youtube.com/embed/uM8FJ_qqyMU) provides the original visual context for this rivalry simulation.
