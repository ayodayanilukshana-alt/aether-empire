Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Mantis vs Spider physics simulation?
A dual-team rigid-body rivalry where the Mantis delivers powerful punches and the Spider fires web balls through a maze. Parameters like power, frequency, and maze density are fully adjustable.
How is the simulation calculated?
The simulation runs at 60FPS with simple 2D kinematic physics. Mantis and Spider are represented by circle bodies with velocity, collision detection, and energy transfer. Web balls follow ballistic trajectories.
Can I export the telemetry data?
Yes. Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV file containing all KPI values, velocities, collision counts, and simulation parameters.
How do I share a specific scenario?
Adjust the sliders and presets, then click "Copy Scenario Link". The URL will encode your current parameter state, allowing others to reproduce the exact setup.
