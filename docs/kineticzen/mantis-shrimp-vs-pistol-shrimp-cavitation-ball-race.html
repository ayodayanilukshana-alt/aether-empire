Frequently Asked Questions

A cavitation ball race models the shockwave bubbles generated by two of the fastest strikes in the animal kingdom — the mantis shrimp's raptorial appendage and the pistol shrimp's claw snap — as competing rigid bodies travelling through a fluid medium. The simulation tracks bubble population, shockwave magnitude, and total distance to determine the winning team.

Each frame advances the bubbles using a simplified rigid-body integrator with velocity, drag, and cavitation density parameters. Bubble collisions with the chamber walls spawn rapid cavitation events, which increment the cavitation counter and add to the shockwave KPI. The simulation targets 60 FPS using a fixed-timestep accumulator.

The export produces a comma-separated log of every recorded frame: tick, time, mantis velocity, mantis position, mantis cavitations, pistol velocity, pistol position, pistol cavitations, shockwave, and total cavitation count. It can be opened in any spreadsheet or data-science tool for further analysis.

Presets adjust velocity, cavitation density, viscosity, and bubble radius to represent different physical regimes. CLASSIC is a balanced duel, HIGH SPEED pushes both velocities to extremes, DENSE MEDIUM increases fluid resistance and cavitation density, and CAVITATION CHAOS maximizes bubble spawning and shockwave behavior for stress-testing.

The 800×500 resolution provides a wide horizontal race track with enough vertical space for multiple bubble lanes. The canvas scales responsively to the container width while the internal coordinate system stays fixed, keeping physics consistent across screens.
