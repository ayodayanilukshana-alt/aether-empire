How is the race physics modeled?

Each ball is simulated with a simplified rigid-body integration: gravity, surface friction, and a preset initial impulse. The Volcano ball has a higher mass (2.4 kg) but lower base friction, while Glacier is lighter (1.8 kg) with slightly higher friction. The slider values adjust global parameters in real time.

What do the presets change?

Presets alter the initial velocity and friction coefficients. "Volcano Boost" increases Volcano's initial impulse, "Glacier Boost" gives Glacier a head start, "Chaos" adds random perturbations to both, and "Balanced" restores the default competitive setup.
