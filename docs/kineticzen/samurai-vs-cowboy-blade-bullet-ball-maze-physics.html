How does the maze physics simulation work?

The canvas uses rigid-body ball dynamics with adjustable gravity, restitution, and collision forces. Samurai (red) and Cowboy (yellow) projectiles bounce through a procedural maze, with real-time telemetry.

What do the sliders control?

Blade Force scales Samurai impact energy. Bullet Speed scales Cowboy projectile velocity. Gravity and Restitution affect all bodies. Presets instantly load balanced scenarios.

Can I export the live collision data?

Yes — press "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV containing every collision event, current KPI values, and slider settings. The link button copies the full scenario URL.
