FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the Dino Ball Maze? +
A 60FPS rigid-body physics simulation where T-Rex and Spino compete as balls navigating a maze, using Matter.js engine dynamics.
How is the winner determined? +
The team with the higher score (maze progress + collision points) at the end of the simulation wins. Scores update in real-time.
Can I export telemetry data? +
Yes, click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV file containing all live metrics for both teams.
What do the sliders control? +
Gravity, mass, friction, and restitution affect the balls' movement, collisions, and overall race dynamics. Adjust them to create different outcomes.
