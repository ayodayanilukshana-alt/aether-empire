❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Turbo Ball Maze simulate?

It models a 60 FPS rigid-body physics duel where Mario (heavier, more friction) and Sonic (lighter, higher restitution) collide in a cosmic maze. The canvas visualizes real-time collision impulses.

How are the KPIs calculated?

Impulse = mass × velocity change. Friction is derived from mass and restitution. Collision rate is a function of maze geometry and gravity. All values update live with the sliders.

Can I export the scenario?

Yes — the "Export CSV Telemetry" button downloads a CSV with all current physics parameters and KPI values. "Copy Scenario Link" copies a URL that encodes your slider settings.

What do the preset buttons do?

Presets instantly set all sliders to known configurations: Balanced, Sonic Speed (low mass, high gravity), Mario Heavy (high mass, high friction), and Chaos (extreme values).
