Frequently Asked Questions

Operational details for the TESLA vs SPACEX physics workstation.
What does this simulation actually model?
A 60FPS rigid-body solver in which two sphere classes — TESLA (red) and SPACEX (blue) — are launched into a kinetic arena. Each body obeys gravity, restitution, and per-team thrust. Bodies that cross the top escape line are counted as escapes; the leader is the team with more escapes.
How is the escape velocity determined?
Escape is triggered when the body's center crosses the horizontal escape zone at the top of the canvas. Effective escape velocity is a function of gravity, per-team thrust, and cumulative collision impulse. Higher SPACEX thrust yields faster ascent; higher TESLA mass yields more collision momentum transfer.
What do the presets change?
Each preset rewrites gravity, restitution, both thrust values, and launch rate simultaneously: Standard (balanced), TESLA Boost (heavy red bias), SPACEX Boost (blue thrust bias), Zero-G (no gravity, pure momentum), Chaos (max restitution, high rate), Dense Arena (high gravity, dense spawn rate).
Can I export the telemetry?
Yes. "Export CSV Telemetry" writes a timestamped CSV containing all settings, KPI snapshots, per-team escape counts, active body counts, and kinetic energy at export time. Use it for comparative scenario analysis.
Are scenario links reproducible?
Yes. "Copy Scenario Link" serializes all slider values and toggle states into the URL hash. Opening that URL restores the exact scenario, so a shared link reproduces the same physics setup frame-for-frame from launch.
