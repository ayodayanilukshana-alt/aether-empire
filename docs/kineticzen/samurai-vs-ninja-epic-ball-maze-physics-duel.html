What exactly is being simulated?
Two ball agents — the SAMURAI steel sphere and the NINJA shuriken orb — navigate a vertical baffle maze under gravity. Each frame runs fixed 1/60 s substeps with gravity, linear friction, restitution on baffle impacts, wall collisions, and agent-agent elastic collisions with a distribution driven by the ball mass ratio.
How are the KPIs computed?
Samurai/Ninja Speed is the per-frame displacement magnitude in px/frame. Duel Kinetic Energy is ½·m·v² summed over both agents (mass normalised to 1.0 for the Samurai, ratio-scaled for the Ninja). Baffle Collisions counts restitution events on maze baffles. Maze Progress is vertical descent as a percentage of the maze span, and Lead is the gap between the leader's progress and the trailing agent.
Does changing the sliders mid-duel work?
Yes. Gravity, friction, restitution, tilt, dodge skill and time scale are read every frame, so the duel responds instantly. Ball mass ratio applies on the next agent-agent collision. Changing maze baffles rebuilds the arena geometrically and resets positions.
What does "AI Dodge Skill" do?
It blends each agent's steering between a pure gravity descent and a lateral evasive impulse away from the nearest baffle or rival. At 0 the agents fall almost straight; at 1 they aggressively weave, which increases baffle collisions and kinetic energy but lowers average descent speed in tight mazes.
What is included in the CSV export?
A header block with scenario name, all active parameters, and derived arena geometry, followed by one row per sampled frame: frame index, time, both agents' x/y/velocity, kinetic energy, cumulative baffle collisions, progress percentages and the current leader. Sampled at 6 Hz of simulated time to keep files manageable.
Is the scenario link shareable?
Yes. "Copy Scenario Link" serialises every slider plus the selected preset into the URL hash. Opening the link restores that exact configuration and auto-starts the duel, so the run is reproducible.
