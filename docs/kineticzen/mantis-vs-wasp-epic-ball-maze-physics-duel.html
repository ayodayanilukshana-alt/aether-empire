❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is the MANTIS vs WASP ball maze simulation?
It's a physics-based rivalry model where two autonomous balls (team MANTIS and team WASP) navigate a procedurally generated maze. Sliders let you alter gravity, friction, restitution, and maze complexity — all computed in real time with canvas rendering.
How is the telemetry generated?
We use a custom lightweight physics loop (inspired by Matter.js) that tracks collisions, velocity, energy, and path efficiency for each team. Every simulation step updates the KPIs and the analytical table.
Can I save or share my scenario?
Yes — use the "Copy Scenario Link" button to copy a URL that encodes your current slider values. "Export CSV Telemetry" downloads a snapshot of all live KPIs and analytical table data.
Is this the same physics as the video?
The video features a Matter.js rigid-body duel. Our simulation is an interactive approximation with the same teams and theme. It runs at 60 FPS and uses similar physics concepts.
