FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What does this simulation model? +
A 60 FPS rigid-body physics race between PUMA and NIKE balls. You control mass, restitution, and gravity to see how each parameter affects velocity, displacement, and energy retention.
How do I export telemetry? +
Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV containing the current parameter set, peak velocities, and energy retention for both teams.
Can I share my scenario? +
Yes. Click "Copy Scenario Link" to copy a URL that encodes your current slider settings. Anyone who opens it will see the same configuration.
Why do the balls bounce differently? +
PUMA and NIKE have independent restitution values. Higher restitution means more elastic collisions and greater energy retention.
