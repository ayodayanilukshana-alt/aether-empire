FAQ · Simulation Mechanics

What is the physics model behind Lightning vs Tornado?
We simulate two rigid-body agents (Lightning, mass=2.0; Tornado, mass=3.5) inside a circular maze arena. The Lightning agent receives a repulsive charge force from the center, while the Tornado agent applies a tangential spin force. Gravity and wall collisions are resolved at 60 FPS.

How are presets defined?
Presets modify the force, spin, and gravity parameters: STANDARD (5.0/5.0/1.0), CHARGED (9.0/3.0/1.2), VORTEX (2.0/8.5/0.6), CHAOS (8.0/8.0/1.8).

Can I export telemetry data?
Yes — the "Export CSV Telemetry" button generates a CSV with position, velocity, kinetic energy, and collision count for both agents over the last 300 frames.

What does "Copy Scenario Link" do?
It copies a URL with all current slider values encoded in the hash, so you can share the exact simulation state.
