03 Frequently Asked Questions
What does the simulation model exactly?

A rigid-body physics rivalry where a molten volcanic ball (high density, low friction) navigates a glacier ice maze (high friction, elastic collisions). The sliders control mass, gravity, friction, restitution, and maze gap width in real time.

How is the 60FPS achieved without a physics engine library?

This workstation uses a custom lightweight integrator running at requestAnimationFrame, with fixed-substep collision resolution. All calculations are performed on the CPU and rendered to a single canvas element for maximum compatibility.

Can I export the telemetry data?

Yes. The "Export CSV Telemetry" button downloads a fully formatted CSV containing all current parameters, KPIs, and derived metrics. The "Copy Scenario Link" button generates a shareable URL with encoded slider values.

What do the scenario presets do?

BLIZZARD increases friction and restitution, ERUPTION increases gravity and mass, and VACUUM sets gravity near zero and friction minimal. Each preset updates all sliders and the simulation immediately.
