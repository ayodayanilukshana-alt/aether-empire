FAQ
What physics engine is used?

A custom 2D rigid-body implementation modeled after Matter.js, running at 60FPS with iterative constraint solving and continuous collision detection.

How do I reproduce a specific scenario?

Adjust the sliders, then click "COPY SCENARIO LINK". The URL hash will encode gravity, restitution, ball count, and maze density.

What does 'MARVEL' vs 'DC' represent?

The two teams are assigned distinct ball colors. The simulation tracks escape velocity and collision counts for each team independently.

Can I export raw data?

Yes — the "EXPORT CSV TELEMETRY" button downloads a CSV containing per-frame velocity, position, and team metrics.
