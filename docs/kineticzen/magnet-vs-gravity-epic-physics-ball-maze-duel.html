FAQ · Magnet vs Gravity
What exactly is being simulated?
A 60 FPS rigid-body ball maze duel where two teams compete for dominance. The MAGNET team uses an attractive force field, while the GRAVITY team relies on a downward acceleration vector. Score is determined by which team's balls occupy the opposing zone longer.
How are the teams color-coded?
MAGNET balls are rendered in the KINETICZEN accent (#f43f5e), and GRAVITY balls in institutional blue (#60a5fa).
Can I export the telemetry data?
Yes. The "Export CSV Telemetry" button generates a real-time CSV snapshot containing all physics parameters, scores, and collision data.
What do the sliders control?
Magnetic Strength scales the attractive force toward the magnet's center. Gravity Force scales the downward acceleration. Restitution controls bounciness, and Ball Count sets the total entities in the maze.
How do I share a scenario?
Click "Copy Scenario Link" to copy a URL with current slider states encoded as query parameters. Opening that URL restores your exact configuration.
