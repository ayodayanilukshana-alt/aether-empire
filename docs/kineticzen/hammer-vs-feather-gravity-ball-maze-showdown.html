Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the hammer fall faster than the feather?
In a vacuum, both fall identically. With air resistance, the feather's high surface-area-to-mass ratio creates significant drag, while the hammer's dense mass overwhelms it. This simulation lets you adjust gravity and drag to see the crossover point.
What does the 'Maze Complexity' slider do?
It procedurally modifies the internal maze geometry, adding or removing obstacle rows and switchbacks. Higher complexity increases path length and collision count, exaggerating the time delta between hammer and feather.
How is the CSV telemetry generated?
On each completed run, the simulation captures per-frame position, velocity, and collision events. The export button serializes these into a standard CSV file with timestamped rows for both bodies.
Can I reproduce the exact video conditions?
Yes. Click the "Vacuum" preset to remove air resistance, then set gravity to 9.8 m/s². Both bodies will complete the maze in nearly identical times, showcasing the equivalence principle.
