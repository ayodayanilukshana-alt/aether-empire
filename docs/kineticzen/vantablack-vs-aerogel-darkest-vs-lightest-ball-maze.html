FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is the Vantablack vs Aerogel maze simulation?

It is a 60FPS physics rivalry where a Vantablack sphere (ultra-dark, high restitution) and an Aerogel sphere (ultra-light, low density) compete in a maze. The simulation uses Matter.js rigid-body dynamics with adjustable gravity, friction, and elasticity.

How do I export telemetry?

Click the "Export CSV Telemetry" button to download a CSV containing current slider values, ball positions, velocities, collision counts, and timestamps. The data is captured at the moment of export.

Can I share my scenario?

Yes! The "Copy Scenario Link" button copies a URL with query parameters that encode your current gravity, restitution, and friction settings. Anyone opening the link will see the same physics configuration.

What is the difference between the preset buttons?

Presets adjust the physics sliders: Standard (gravity 0.8, restitution 0.7, friction 0.3), Low-G (gravity 0.2, restitution 0.9, friction 0.1), Bouncy (gravity 1.0, restitution 0.95, friction 0.05), Sticky (gravity 1.2, restitution 0.2, friction 0.75), and Chaos (randomized gravity, restitution, and friction).
