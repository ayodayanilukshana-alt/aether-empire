❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Fire vs Ice physics simulation?
This is a rigid-body racing simulation where two spheres (Fire and Ice) collide, bounce, and race across a bounded arena under custom physics parameters. It models mass, friction, restitution, and kinetic energy transfer in real time.
How are the physics parameters used?
Mass affects momentum and collision response. Friction controls energy loss during sliding. Restitution determines bounciness. Adjusting these sliders changes the entire simulation behavior instantly.
Can I export the telemetry data?
Yes. Click "Export CSV Telemetry" to download a CSV file containing current velocities, energies, momenta, and simulation settings for both Fire and Ice.
What does the "Copy Scenario Link" button do?
It copies a URL with encoded simulation parameters (mass, friction, restitution) to your clipboard so you can share the exact scenario with others.
